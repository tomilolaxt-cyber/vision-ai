HOW TO INSTALL VISION AI ON YOUR LG TV
=======================================

METHOD 1 - DEVELOPER MODE (Recommended)
-----------------------------------------
1. On your LG TV remote, go to Settings
2. Go to "General" -> "About This TV"  
3. Click the TV software version number 5 times fast
   -> This enables Developer Mode

4. Download "LG webOS Dev Manager" on your laptop from:
   https://webostv.developer.lge.com/develop/tools/webos-studio

5. Connect TV and laptop to same WiFi

6. In Dev Manager, click "Device Manager" -> Add your TV IP (192.168.1.11)

7. Click "Install" and select this lg-app folder

8. Vision AI will appear on your TV home screen!


METHOD 2 - DIRECT BROWSER (Works RIGHT NOW, no install needed)
---------------------------------------------------------------
1. On your LG TV, press HOME button on remote
2. Find "Web Browser" app (or LG Browser)
3. Type this address: http://192.168.100.136:5000/tv-app
4. Vision AI loads full screen on your TV!
5. Use your remote's arrow keys and OK button to navigate


IMPORTANT:
- Your laptop must be running server.py
- Both TV and laptop must be on same WiFi
- Laptop IP: 192.168.100.136
- TV IP: 192.168.1.11
